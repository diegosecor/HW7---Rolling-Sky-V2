HOW TO READ THE GARDEN

Steer with ← →. Jump with ↑. Forward movement is automatic.

Gold sphere: you. Cyan diamonds: +100 points. Amber corners: solid hazards. Striped dark breaks: gaps to jump or avoid.

Grass and carved mosaics are safe floor. Tall bushes, stones, crystals and ducks must be dodged, even during a jump. Nightfall dims the garden; crosswinds shift the track.

Every garden is open. Clear a route to earn its atlas stamp, then continue to the next. Your current level is marked in gold; cleared gardens have a stamp. After a win, the large button names your next level. Enter continues; Space replays. P pauses.
